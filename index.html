Our Approach

Story first, always.

Most estate sales feel transactional. We treat them like what they actually are — the closing chapter of someone's home, handled with care. The people we work with are often clearing out a family home, a parent's collection, or forty years of someone's life, and that deserves more than a walkthrough with a clipboard.

We take the time to listen to the story behind a collection before we touch a single box. It's a small difference in approach, but it changes how the whole process feels — for the family letting go, and for whoever finds these pieces next.

“ Forty years of someone's life doesn't fit in a dumpster. ”
