Estate Liquidation · Connecticut

Estate liquidation in Connecticut, handled start to finish.

Hestern Auctions is an online estate auction house in Prospect, CT. We liquidate full estates, family homes, and inherited collections through documented online auctions that reach bidders well beyond Connecticut.

What we handle

Most of the families we work with are clearing a parent's home, settling an estate, or sorting through a lifetime of belongings. We take on full-house estates as well as individual collections and standout pieces, and we manage the process so you're not doing it alone.

How an estate liquidation works with Hestern

  • Walkthrough. We visit in person, listen to the story behind the collection, and identify what's worth featuring.
  • Documentation. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and researched so it's presented accurately to bidders.
  • Online auction. Your lots go live to our national bidder base on AuctionNinja, with active promotion through the auction window.
  • Settlement. We settle up promptly after the auction closes, with a clear accounting of every item sold.

After the sale, we also offer clean-out and cleaning services so the space is ready to hand off, list, or move into.

What sells at our auctions

Recent Hestern auctions have included antique clocks and a drop-front desk, a vintage Ford Mustang and a Honda S2000, Howard Miller pieces, American Girl dolls, glassware and art, tufted furniture, and preseason skis. See more under Upcoming Auctions.

Where we work

We're based in Prospect, CT and have run auctions across Prospect, Waterbury, Brookfield, Monroe, Stamford, Bloomfield, Watertown, Sandy Hook, and West Haven, Connecticut.

Common questions

How does an online estate auction work?

Your items are photographed, catalogued, and listed for bidding on AuctionNinja. Bidders place bids online during the auction window, and winners pick up their items on a scheduled day. Hestern manages the process from walkthrough to settlement.

When do consignors get paid?

Settlement typically goes out one to two weeks after the auction closes, with an accounting of every item sold.

Does Hestern buy estates outright?

Most estates are consigned to online auction, where bidding determines the final value based on real market demand. In certain cases, we also offer to buy a seller's items outright instead of consigning them. Contact us to talk through your options.

Is Hestern Auctions related to Dave Hester?

No. Hestern Auctions is an independent Connecticut company and is not affiliated with Dave Hester Auctions.
